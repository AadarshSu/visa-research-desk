    How a plan is made

    1. 01

      Finds the official sites

      Only pages on domains confirmed as the destination government's own are read, plus the EU's for a Schengen visa decision.

    2. 02

      Reads the pages live

      The pages that answer your trip are fetched and read. A page that refuses automated reading is named with its link, never guessed at.

    3. 03

      Writes a cited plan

      Whether you need a visa, where to apply and the steps, each linked to its page. What could not be confirmed stays open; with too little evidence, no plan is given.

    Research aid only. Requirements can change; confirm everything with the responsible authority before applying. A visa never guarantees entry or approval.
